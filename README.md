# Home Bias and International Risk Sharing

## Overview

This project investigates the relationship between **home bias** and **international risk sharing** using panel data for OECD countries. It replicates the main findings of the paper:

> Sørensen, B. E., Wu, Y.-T., Yosha, O., & Zhu, Y. **Home Bias and International Risk Sharing: Twin Puzzles Separated at Birth.**

## Data

The analysis uses data from the following sources:

- Foreign equity, debt, and foreign direct investment (FDI) asset and liability data from **Lane and Milesi-Ferretti (2022)**.
- Domestic and international debt securities outstanding from the **Bank for International Settlements (BIS) Quarterly Review**.
- Gross Domestic Product (GDP), Gross National Income (GNI), population, household consumption, and stock market capitalization from the **World Development Indicators (WDI)** for the period **1993–2004**.
